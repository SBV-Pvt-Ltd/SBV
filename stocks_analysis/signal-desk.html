Settings

Price and news lookups need a proxy to reach Yahoo Finance and Google News from the browser (GitHub Pages only serves static files, so it can't do this itself). Deploy the included Cloudflare Worker (cloudflare-worker-proxy.js — free, no CLI, instructions are in the file) and paste its URL below, e.g. https://signal-desk-proxy.you.workers.dev/?url=.

Until that's set, or if it's ever unreachable, the app falls back to a couple of free public proxies — but those are shared, unauthenticated, and go down or get rate-limited without warning, so treat them as a backup, not the plan.

The password below is checked against a hash baked into this file. Anyone who can view the page source can find and replace it, so treat this as a light deterrent — for real access control, put this behind a server-side login.
